# Space Object Categories

## Payload

An object launched with an active operational purpose: communications,
Earth observation, navigation, science, human spaceflight, etc.

## Rocket Body

Spent launch vehicle stages left in orbit after delivering their payloads.
They are large (often 10+ meters), long-lived, and represent a significant
fraction of massive tracked debris.

## Debris

Fragments and retired hardware with no independent operational mission,
including explosion fragments and items released unintentionally.
They remain operationally relevant because they can participate in
conjunctions with active spacecraft.

## Why typing matters for risk

An encounter involving an operational payload usually warrants more
attention than one between two inert objects, because collision would cause
service loss and potentially create new debris. Orbital Guardian includes
object type as one factor of its priority score and never fabricates mission
details for unverified objects.
